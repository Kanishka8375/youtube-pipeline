# Weekly Review Dashboard

The Sunday surface. Twenty to thirty minutes, once a week, and it should end with one
decision — not a pile of numbers.

---

## Page structure

```
Weekly Review Dashboard
├── Header + quick-add buttons
├── Section 1  Current Week Snapshot
├── Section 2  KPI scorecards (long-form / Shorts / monetization)
├── Section 3  Weekly summary notes
├── Section 4  This week's long-form videos
├── Section 5  This week's Shorts
├── Section 6  Packaging review
├── Section 7  Monetization review
├── Section 8  Repackaging candidates
├── Section 9  Next week decisions
└── Section 10 Weekly Reviews archive
```

## Section 1 — Current Week Snapshot

A linked view of **Weekly Reviews**, filtered to the latest entry, shown as a gallery card.

Properties on the card: Week · Weekly Score · Weekly Rating · Long-Form Published ·
Shorts Published · Total Views · Total Watch Time Hours · Avg Long-Form CTR ·
Avg Long-Form Retention 30s · Avg Shorts Completion Rate · Total Affiliate Clicks ·
Subscribers Gained · Best Long-Form Video · Best Short · Next Week Focus

## Section 2 — KPI scorecards

Three columns of callout blocks reading from the current week's row.

| Long-form | Shorts | Monetization |
|---|---|---|
| Avg Long-Form CTR | Total Shorts Views | Total Affiliate Clicks |
| Avg Long-Form Retention 30s | Avg Shorts Viewed vs Swiped | Total Conversions |
| Avg Long-Form View Duration | Avg Shorts Completion Rate | Revenue |
| Total Long-Form Views | Best Short views | Best monetizing video |
| Total Watch Time Hours | Shorts Published | Subscribers Gained |

Alongside them, the six score properties: CTR Score · Retention Score · Shorts Score ·
Monetization Score · Consistency Score · **Weekly Score / Weekly Rating**.

## Section 3 — Weekly summary notes

A toggle containing the writing template. Wins, losses, one lesson, one focus. The
template body is in [08-copy-paste-templates.md](08-copy-paste-templates.md).

## Section 4 — This week's long-form videos

Linked view of **Video Pipeline**, table.

- **Filter:** Publish Date is within the past week
- **Sort:** Views 7d, descending
- **Columns:** Title · Publish Date · Content Type · CTR · Impressions · Views 24h ·
  Views 7d · Retention 30s · Avg View Duration · Watch Time Hours · Affiliate Clicks ·
  Comments · Overall Rating · Next Action

**Questions to answer for each video:** did the title earn the click? did the thumbnail
help or hurt? did the first 30 seconds hold? where did viewers drop? did it match viewer
intent? did it drive affiliate clicks?

## Section 5 — This week's Shorts

Linked view of **Shorts Queue**, table.

- **Filter:** Publish Date is within the past week
- **Sort:** Completion Rate, descending
- **Columns:** Hook / Title · Publish Date · Related Long-Form · Views · Viewed vs Swiped ·
  Completion Rate · Likes · Comments · Shares · Performance Rating · Follow-Up Opportunity

**Questions:** which hook pattern won? which Short held best? which topic deserves a
long-form? which failed on the hook rather than the topic?

## Section 6 — Packaging review

Linked view of **Packaging Tracker**, filtered to the past week.

**Columns:** Final Title · Title Formula · Title Angle · Thumbnail Style · Thumbnail Text ·
CTR 24h · CTR 7d · Impressions 7d · Winner Rating · Repackaged?

Below it, the weekly packaging checklist from
[05-packaging-tracker.md](05-packaging-tracker.md#weekly-packaging-review).

## Section 7 — Monetization review

Linked view of **Affiliate Tracker**: Tool Name · Promoted In Videos · Clicks ·
Conversions · Revenue · Best Placement · Notes.

**Questions:** which tool got the most clicks? which video drove the most affiliate
traffic? which CTA placement worked? do comparisons convert better than roundups?

## Section 8 — Repackaging candidates

The most useful view on the page: videos where the content works but the packaging
doesn't.

Linked view of **Video Pipeline** or **Packaging Tracker**:

- **Filter:** `CTR` < 4.5 **and** `Retention 30s` > 60 **and** `Repackaged?` is unchecked
- Add `Impressions` > 1000 so brand-new videos don't appear before their numbers mean
  anything
- **Columns:** Title · CTR · Impressions · Retention 30s · Avg View Duration ·
  Thumbnail Style · Title Formula · Next Action

## Section 9 — Next week decisions

Four short lists. Keep each to three items — a list of ten is a list of none.

**Repeat** · **Improve** · **Stop** · **Test**

## Section 10 — Weekly Reviews archive

Linked view of **Weekly Reviews**, all weeks: Week · Total Views · Avg Long-Form CTR ·
Avg Shorts Completion Rate · Total Affiliate Clicks · Weekly Score · Weekly Rating ·
Next Week Focus.

Plus two filtered views:
- **Best Weeks** — Weekly Score ≥ 18
- **Problem Weeks** — Weekly Score < 14, or Repackaging Needed is checked

## The Sunday routine

1. Open the dashboard
2. Review long-form metrics
3. Review Shorts metrics
4. Review the packaging tracker
5. Write three wins and three losses
6. Decide what to repeat
7. Write one test for next week

**Time target: 20–30 minutes.** If it regularly runs past 45, you are tracking too much.
Cut back to the lean set below.

---

## Lean version

If the full dashboard is too much to maintain, track only these and drop the rest. It
keeps roughly all of the signal:

**Long-form:** CTR · Retention 30s · Avg View Duration · Affiliate Clicks
**Shorts:** Views · Viewed vs Swiped · Completion Rate
**Strategy:** best topic · best title style · best thumbnail style · next week's test

A lean tracker you fill in every week beats a complete one you abandon in month two.
