## Script Template

The one structure to reuse for every video. Familiar structure is what makes a 90-minute
scripting block realistic.

```markdown
## Hook — 0:00–0:15
One sentence naming the audience and the result.
No channel intro, no "hey guys", no logo sting.

## Promise — 0:15–0:30
What they will be able to do by the end, and roughly how long it takes.
Name the tools you will cover.

## Section 1 — the obvious pick
Tool, what it does, one real demo, who it is for.

## Section 2 — the better pick
Tool, what it does, one real demo, how it differs from Section 1.

## Section 3 — the nuance
Where these tools fail, what they cost, who should not use them.
This section is what makes the rest credible.

## Verdict
Direct recommendation. Name one tool. Do not hedge.

## CTA
One ask only: watch the next video, or grab the link below.
```

Script QA before recording: clear audience · clear result · no fluff · real examples ·
affiliate mention feels natural · strong first 30 seconds.

```markdown
commission at no extra cost to you. It does not affect which tools I recommend.

[link]

```

Keep the disclosure line fixed and never remove it from the template. It is an FTC
requirement in the US, and YouTube's paid-promotion checkbox is a separate step that
also needs ticking.
